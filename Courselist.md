**Purpose:**
This document records the courses and their lecture/practical requirements extracted from the official Computer Science Department provisional timetables for the 2025/2026 academic session.

**Legend:**

* **L** = Lecture
* **P** = Practical
* **MSQ** = Mandatory Skills Qualification
* **1L/1P** = One lecture + one practical
* **1L/0P** = One lecture only
* **0L/1P** = Practical only

# 8. DATABASE REFERENCE FORMAT

For the AUTO-TIMETABLE project, each course requirement should ultimately follow this structure:

```text
course_code
level
semester
programme
lectures_per_week
practicals_per_week
```

Examples:

```text
COM111 | ND1 | First Semester  | NULL | 1 | 1
COM121 | ND1 | Second Semester | NULL | 1 | 1

COM211 | ND2 | First Semester  | NULL | 1 | 1
COM221 | ND2 | Second Semester | NULL | 1 | 1
```
